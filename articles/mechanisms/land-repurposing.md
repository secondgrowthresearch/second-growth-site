## What the program does

The Multibenefit Land Repurposing Program (MLRP) is administered by the **California Department of Conservation** — not DWR, a standing correction from this project's earlier research, which had the agency wrong. It funds converting irrigated farmland facing retirement under SGMA into a different, still-productive land use: habitat, groundwater recharge, flood management, or similar. Its awarded-projects list names four grants, each for $8,890,000, totaling $35,560,000.

## The one award that touches these four counties

Of the four MLRP awards, only one is tied to this project's geography by name: **Westlands Water District GSA**, covering the Westside subbasin, which spans Kings and Fresno counties. The other three — Pajaro Valley Water Management Agency, the Merced Subbasin GSA, and the East Turlock Subbasin GSA — sit outside this project's four-county scope (Merced and East Turlock are adjacent but distinct subbasins from Stanislaus's own GSAs).

## Why we can't build a $/acre figure for it

Every other mechanism on these pages has, or could be built toward, a real cost-per-acre or cost-per-acre-foot figure. This one can't yet: the MLRP awarded-projects list gives no acreage or per-project land area for any of the four grants, including Westlands'. Real money, real in-county geography, no project-level accounting detail — the same shape of gap this project has already found in recharge-basin O&M and other areas, logged honestly rather than filled with an estimate.

This is the one mechanism on these five pages whose closest federal match is currently non-functional. USDA's Farm Service Agency runs the Conservation Reserve Program (CRP), which pays to retire marginal cropland — the clearest federal cognate to what MLRP does at the state level. **CRP's statutory authority lapsed on September 30, 2026, and new enrollment is paused as of this writing.** The House has passed a reauthorization bill (H.R. 7567, which would restore CRP at 27 million acres nationally through FY2031) on a bipartisan 224–200 vote; the Senate's companion bill's floor status is unconfirmed in what this research could find. NRCS's Agricultural Conservation Easement Program — Agricultural Land Easements component (ACEP-ALE) is a smaller, still-active federal alternative, cost-sharing 50–75% of an easement's value on working land, though it protects farmland rather than funding its conversion to a different use the way CRP and MLRP do.

## What we don't know

- Acreage or per-acre figures for any of the four MLRP awards, including Westlands' — the awarded-projects list doesn't provide them.
- Whether the Senate has moved on its own farm bill reauthorization since this research was done, or what a conference agreement between the House and Senate versions would look like on CRP specifically.
- Whether any Kings or Fresno County GSA besides Westlands has applied for or been denied an MLRP award.
