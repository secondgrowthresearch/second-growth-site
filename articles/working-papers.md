# Working Papers

Most of this site answers a reader's question directly, built for someone who wants to understand the Central Valley's economy without needing a research background. These pages serve a different purpose: a formal, self-contained writeup of the same underlying work, in a format a researcher, reviewer, or outside collaborator can read and cite on its own.

- **[Do Plant Closures Show Up in County-Level Economic Data?](working-papers/sdi-synthetic-control-2026.md)** — a synthetic-control test of two real Central Valley plant closures, synthesizing findings already documented across [the Index](the-index.md), [Measuring Closures](synthetic-control.md), and [Methodology](methodology.md) into one citable paper.
- **[Does MLRP's Claimed Employment Co-Benefit Reach the Farm Labor It Displaces?](working-papers/mlrp-methodology-memo-2026.md)** — a methodology memo proposing how to test whether California's Multibenefit Land Repurposing Program's claimed "Employment Opportunities" co-benefit actually reaches the farm labor it displaces, extending this project's own synthetic-control findings to a live state program.
