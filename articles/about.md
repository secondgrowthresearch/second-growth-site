# About Second Growth

Second Growth is a research organization that documents consolidation: what happens to a place's people and economy when the single industry that organized it concentrates into fewer hands — fewer owners working the same land, the water and the labor concentrating alongside them, and a region that keeps growing while the ownership of it does not.

We work from primary data and named sources. Where the existing record has gaps, we commission or conduct original research to close them. Our goal is a public, checkable evidence record — not an advocacy narrative — that researchers, agencies, and the communities affected can all work from.

## Our name

"Second growth" is a real forestry term: the regrowth that follows logging or fire, often different and more diverse than what grew there first. We chose it because it describes what we're actually studying — what grows back after a single, dominant system has worked a place over, and in whose hands — without assuming in advance what that should look like.

## Current work

Our current work is the methodology and evidence base for our first case study: California's Central Valley, where population and employment have each grown by roughly 60% since 1990 — and where, over the same decades, commodity-agriculture and food-processing ownership has concentrated into fewer hands, the water has concentrated with it, and the pay for doing the work has not followed the growth.
