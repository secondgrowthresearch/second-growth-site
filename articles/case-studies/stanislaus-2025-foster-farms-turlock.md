# Foster Farms ends fresh-turkey operations — Turlock, Stanislaus County, 2025

Foster Farms closed its fresh-turkey processing plant in Turlock in May 2025, eliminating at least 478 jobs — the largest Stanislaus County closure found outside Del Monte, and the first poultry-sector closure in this project's record.

## What happened

Foster Farms announced in late January 2025 that it would end fresh-turkey processing at its Turlock complex, with operations ceasing May 9, 2025. The company cited "market demand changes" and its "ongoing strategic plan" — no further detail was given in any source found.

The closure wasn't confined to one building. The official WARN filing lists 478 employees at the main F Street plant, with two smaller companion filings — 25 and 9 employees — covering a live-turkey trucking yard on C Street and a feed mill on West Main Street, both part of the same fresh-turkey operation. Press coverage puts the combined total at 519. Foster Farms' adjacent cooked-products plant in Turlock was not affected and continues operating — this is a partial exit from one product line, not a full site shutdown.

Workers were offered the option to transfer into openings at other Central Valley Foster Farms facilities that produce fully-cooked poultry products. Stanislaus County Workforce Development engaged with the company on job fairs and orientation services for those affected.

## Impact — what we know, and what we don't

- **Jobs:** 478 confirmed by WARN filing at the main site; 519 is the company's own total across all three affected locations.
- **What happened to the workers:** Beyond the transfer offer and county workforce outreach, not documented — no source found tracks how many workers actually moved to other Foster Farms sites versus were laid off outright.
- **Ownership context, not yet connected to the closure by any source:** Foster Farms was acquired by private-equity firm Atlas Holdings in July 2022. Whether that ownership change bears on this decision is unestablished — noted as a fact, not a claim.
- **Unverified claim, flagged not used:** one outlet reported that Tyson Foods would relocate the Turlock turkey operation to Livingston, about 10 miles south. No second source confirms this — treat as unverified.
- **Update, Nov 2025:** Diestel Family Ranch, a Sonora-area specialty turkey producer, bought the closed F Street plant and announced plans to reopen it in early 2026, processing raw turkey products (ground turkey, burgers, roasts) with a different, much smaller workforce — the company said it would start with "a couple dozen" employees and grow from there. That's a real but partial recovery: a fraction of the 519 jobs lost, under new ownership, not a reversal of the original closure.

## Part of a larger pattern

A systematic review of every Fresno, Stanislaus, and Tulare County WARN filing since 2014 — not just news search — surfaced this closure alongside three others landing on Stanislaus County within about 21 months: [Tropicale Foods' Modesto ice-cream plant](stanislaus-2024-tropicale-foods-modesto.md) (July 2024, 168 jobs), [Reyes Coca-Cola Bottling](stanislaus-2024-reyes-coca-cola-modesto.md) (Nov 2024, 101 jobs), this one (Jan 2025), and [Del Monte's Modesto/Hughson canneries](stanislaus-2026-del-monte-modesto-hughson.md) (April 2026, 765+ jobs). The same compounding pattern already documented in Kings County — multiple sequential exits landing on the same small set of towns — shows up independently in Stanislaus.
